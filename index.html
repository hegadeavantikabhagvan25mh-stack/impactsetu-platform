How the matching actually works

01

Read public data

Census, NFHS and district-level indicators are combined into a live map of underserved needs.

02

Verify NGOs

Registration (12A / 80G / CSR-1) and track record are checked before an NGO is listed.

03

Match by fit

Cause area, geography and credibility are scored to rank the strongest matches on both sides.

04

Feed the loop

Funded NGOs report outcomes back, sharpening the need-map for everyone who comes after.
