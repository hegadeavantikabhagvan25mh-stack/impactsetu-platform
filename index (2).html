How the matching actually works

01

Read public data

NFHS-5 district-level indicators are combined into a live map of underserved needs (Census and NITI Aayog data planned next).

02

Identify NGOs

Real organisations are identified using public information. Formal registration checks (12A, 80G, CSR-1) are planned for later, not done yet for the current directory.

03

Match by fit

Cause area, geography and credibility are scored to rank the strongest matches on both sides.

04

Feed the loop

Funded NGOs report outcomes back, sharpening the need-map for everyone who comes after.
